1. EVENT OVERVIEW
Hack The Future 3.0
Hack The Future 3.0 is a national-level hackathon designed to bring together students, innovators, developers, designers and aspiring entrepreneurs from across India.
The third edition introduces a stronger focus on entrepreneurship and product development.
This year's theme is:
PROJECT TO PRODUCT
The objective is to encourage students to move beyond building projects solely for academic or competition purposes and instead develop solutions with the potential to become meaningful, scalable and user-focused products.
Participants will be challenged to identify real-world problems, develop innovative solutions, build functional prototypes, validate their ideas and present their solutions with a clear product and entrepreneurship perspective.


The journey of Hack The Future 3.0 is:
PROBLEM → IDEA → PROJECT → PROTOTYPE → PRODUCT → PITCH
Hack The Future 3.0 is not only about writing code. It is about developing the mindset required to become a builder, innovator, product creator and future entrepreneur.
